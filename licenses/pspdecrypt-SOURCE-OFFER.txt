pspdecrypt source offer
=======================

The EmotionalSense launcher runs `pspdecrypt` (bin/pspdecrypt in the kit) as a
separate program, through subprocess. It is not linked with, and does not
include code from, the rest of EmotionalSense, which is MIT licensed.

pspdecrypt is Copyright (C) its authors and licensed under the GNU General
Public License, version 3 (licenses/pspdecrypt-GPL-3.0.txt).

Corresponding source of the exact revision shipped:

    https://github.com/John-K/pspdecrypt
    revision c156627db7634d395c380c0a9589130f603307fc

Every release also attaches that revision's source archive
(pspdecrypt-src-c156627.tar.gz) next to the launcher. If you received the
binary without it, you may request the source for three years from the
release date by opening an issue at the EmotionalSense repository; it is also
available at the URL above for as long as that repository exists.
